Academic & Open Source Overview

About EcoTrack

Smart Waste Management and Segregation System developed for the University of Mumbai Engineering Mini-Project on Open Source Software Development.

Problem Statement & Vision

Rapid urban population growth across Indian metropolitan areas like Greater Mumbai has resulted in escalating municipal solid waste generation. Inadequate source segregation and delayed response to localized garbage dumping exacerbate environmental pollution, block monsoon drains, and compromise public health.

EcoTrack provides a full-stack, transparent, and accessible civic platform enabling citizens to report garbage hotspots, categorize waste streams (Wet, Dry, Plastic, E-waste, Mixed), visualize civic hotspots via open-source OpenStreetMap layers, and monitor municipal progress from report triage to verified ground resolution.

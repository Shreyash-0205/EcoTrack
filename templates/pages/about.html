Civic Platform Overview

About EcoTrack

A smart waste management and segregation system designed to empower communities and support cleaner urban environments.

Problem Statement & Vision

Rapid urbanization creates severe municipal waste challenges, including unsegregated disposal, overflowing street bins, and delayed cleanup responses. EcoTrack provides a centralized, transparent platform connecting citizens with municipal sanitation teams. Citizens report localized hotspots with waste categorization and coordinates, while administrators triage complaints, assign cleanup crews, and track progress to verified resolution.

Key Features
Waste Reporting

Submit waste hotspots with descriptive landmarks, waste stream classification, optional photos, and precise GPS coordinates via device geolocation or an interactive map pin.

Segregation Guide

Comprehensive educational reference detailing Wet, Dry, Plastic, E-waste, and Hazardous waste streams with practical disposal advice adaptable to local municipal guidelines.

Interactive Hotspot Map

OpenStreetMap integration using Leaflet.js displaying reported hotspots with status-colored pins, popups, and instant filters by category and resolution status.

Dashboard & Report Tracking

Personal dashboard with status tracking (Reported, In Progress, Resolved) and Chart.js analytics, alongside an administrative triage console and immutable audit logs.

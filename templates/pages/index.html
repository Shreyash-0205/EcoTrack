How EcoTrack Operates

A closed-loop civic reporting pipeline that ensures accountability from citizen complaint to verified ground cleanup.

1. Spot & Report

Citizens identify an unattended waste hotspot, drop a GPS pin or landmark, select waste category, and upload an optional photo.

2. Triage & Segregation

Sanitation administrators review submitted reports, assess waste stream categories (Wet, Dry, Plastic, E-waste), and assign dedicated municipal crews.

3. Verified Resolution

Cleanup actions are recorded with administrative notes and audit logs. The hotspot is marked resolved with public status tracking.
